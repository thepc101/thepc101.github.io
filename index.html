(01) About

My programming journey began in 2022. Since then I've founded five products, led teams, and taught AI to hundreds of younger students.

I care about one thing: building technology that genuinely helps people — students who need a mentor, patients who need clarity, anyone trying to understand their own mind a little better.

Most of my time goes to CodeStarters, where I run bootcamps and hackathons and write the AI curriculum, and to Publick, the AI college counselor I co-founded. Everything else is detail.
